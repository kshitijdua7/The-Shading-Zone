    We listen to your needs, style and vision. In your home or on a video call, we look at the rooms, the light and how you actually use the space — then narrow a very large catalogue down to a short list worth considering.

Blinds & shades / Roller Shades

Roller Shades

Clean, minimal and versatile.

A single panel of fabric on a concealed roller. Nothing else. Roller shades are the most direct answer to a window — they disappear into the reveal when raised and give you an uninterrupted plane of colour and texture when lowered.

Book free consultationGet a free quote

The detail

Because the whole shade is one piece of fabric, the fabric choice does all the work. The same mechanism can give you a near-transparent screen that keeps the view and cuts glare, a soft light-filtering weave, or a full blackout panel for a bedroom. We cut and assemble each one to your opening, so the fabric sits square in the frame with even light gaps.

Roller Shades in rooms

Questions

Asked and answered

Will a solar screen give me privacy at night?

No. Solar screens work by contrast — during the day you can see out and passers-by mostly cannot see in, but that reverses once your interior lights are on. For night privacy we usually pair a screen with a blackout shade or drapery.

Can one shade cover a very wide window?

Up to a point. Beyond a certain width the fabric and tube start to deflect, so we either split the opening into two shades on a shared headrail or move you to a panel track system. We'll tell you which applies to your window at the consultation.

How much light comes in around the edges?

An inside-mounted roller always leaves a small light gap at the sides. If you need true darkness — a nursery or a media room — we recommend an outside mount or side channels.

Modern light control with adjustable privacy.

Also called dual or banded shades. Two layers of alternating sheer and opaque bands slide over each other, so you move continuously between a clear view, a filtered glow and full closure — without ever raising the shade.

Zebra Shades in rooms

Questions

Asked and answered

How is this different from a roller shade?

A roller gives you one fabric and one level of light control. A zebra gives you a range — you shift the bands to land anywhere between open and closed without changing the height of the shade.

Do zebra shades block light completely?

Standard ones do not; even with the bands aligned, some light passes through the seams. If you need true darkness, ask us about blackout-band constructions or a separate blackout layer.

Are they good for a bedroom?

They are lovely for daytime living spaces. For bedrooms we usually pair them with drapery or specify a blackout construction.

Questions

Asked and answered

How much of the window does the shade cover when it's up?

The folded stack sits at the top of the opening. Roughly, the taller the shade, the deeper the stack — we can mount above the frame so the stack clears the glass entirely.

Flat or hobbled?

Flat reads as more contemporary and tailored. Hobbled reads softer and more traditional, and it hides an uneven window better. We bring both to the consultation.

Can I get a roman shade in the same fabric as my drapery?

Yes — that's one of the main reasons to choose them. We can make both from the same bolt so the tones match exactly.

Blinds & shades / Cellular Shades

Cellular Shades

Designed for insulation and energy efficiency.

Also known as honeycomb shades. The fabric is folded into hollow cells that trap a layer of air against the glass — the same principle as double glazing, applied to a shade.

Book free consultationGet a free quote

The detail

Cellular shades earn their place on cold windows, hot south-facing rooms and anywhere you are fighting a draft. The trapped air slows heat moving in either direction, and the pleated fabric collapses to a very small stack, so they are also the least visually intrusive product we make. Top-down/bottom-up configurations let you drop the shade from the top for daylight while keeping the lower half closed for privacy.

Features

Cellular Shades in rooms

Questions

Asked and answered

Do cellular shades actually save energy?

They measurably slow heat transfer at the window compared with an uncovered pane, and a double-cell blackout fabric in a tight reveal does more than a single-cell sheer. We won't quote you a percentage — the real number depends on your glazing, orientation and how you use the shade.

What is top-down/bottom-up?

The shade has two moving rails. You can lower the top rail to let daylight in over your head while the bottom stays closed at eye level. It's the best privacy-plus-daylight compromise we sell.

Are they good for a nursery?

Yes — a blackout cellular with side channels is one of the darkest options available, and cordless operation removes the cord hazard entirely.

Venetian Blinds in rooms

Questions

Asked and answered

Aluminium or wood?

Aluminium for kitchens, bathrooms and anywhere with moisture or heat; wood or faux wood for living spaces where you want warmth. Faux wood handles humidity better than real wood.

Can venetians block all the light?

No. Even fully closed, light passes between the slats and around the routing holes. For a dark room, choose a blackout roller, cellular or lined roman instead.

Are they hard to clean?

They need dusting more often than a flat shade — that's the honest trade-off for the adjustability.

Wood & Faux Wood Blinds in rooms

Questions

Asked and answered

Wood or faux wood — which should I choose?

Wood if you want the lightest weight and the real grain. Faux wood if the window is in a kitchen, bathroom or laundry, or gets hard direct sun. We can colour-match across both so a mixed house still looks consistent.

What are cloth tapes for?

They cover the routing holes the lift cords pass through, which removes the row of light pinpoints you'd otherwise see when the slats are closed. They're also a nice decorative detail.

Will wood blinds warp?

Real wood can move in sustained heat or humidity. That's exactly the situation faux wood is for, and we'll say so if your window is one of those.

Motorized Blinds in rooms

Questions

Asked and answered

Do I need to run wiring?

Not necessarily. Battery motors retrofit into an existing home with no electrical work. Hardwiring is cleaner if your walls are already open during a renovation.

Will it work with my smart home?

Where the motor system supports it, yes — integration depends on which motor family we specify and what hub you have. Tell us what you're running and we'll tell you honestly what will and won't connect.

How long do the batteries last?

It depends on the motor, the shade weight and how often it moves, so we won't invent a number. We'll give you the manufacturer's figure for the exact motor we specify.

Is it safe around children?

Motorised coverings have no accessible operating cords, which removes the main hazard. It's one of the reasons we recommend them for nurseries and playrooms.

Usually well above the window frame and close to the ceiling — it makes the window and the room read taller. We'll show you the difference on site.

What is fullness, and does it matter?

It's how much more fabric there is than the width it covers. More fullness means deeper, more even folds. It's the single biggest difference between drapery that looks custom and drapery that looks flat.

Can I layer sheers and blackout?

Yes — a sheer for daytime softness and a blackout behind or in front for night. It's the most flexible thing you can do at a window, and it's very common in bedrooms.

Should the curtains touch the floor?

That's a style choice: a slight break, a kiss to the floor, or a puddle. We'll talk through what suits the room and how you use it.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where a question has no honest general answer — timelines, warranty periods, service areas — we say so rather than inventing one.

16 questions

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes. In-home and virtual consultations are free and carry no obligation. We bring samples, look at your windows and light, and talk through what suits the space.

Do you manufacture your own blinds?

Yes. We design, manufacture, supply and install our window coverings. Your order is built to the measurements we take at your window rather than trimmed down from a stock size.

Do you provide installation?

Yes. Our own installation team fits everything, levels it, demonstrates the operation and takes the packaging away.

Can blinds be completely customized?

Within each product family, yes — dimensions, fabric, colour, opacity, lining, header style, hardware finish, control type and mounting are all specified per window. Some combinations aren't possible on some products, and we'll tell you which when we're with you.

Do you offer motorized blinds?

Yes, across essentially every product family, with battery, plug-in and hardwired motor options and remote, wall or app control where the system supports it.

Can I choose my own fabric and colour?

Yes. We bring a range of samples to the consultation and leave them with you so you can see them in your own light before deciding.

Do you offer blackout options?

Yes — blackout roller shades, blackout cellular shades, blackout-lined roman shades and blackout-lined or interlined drapery. Note that no product blocks light at the edges unless it's outside-mounted or fitted with side channels, and we'll explain that trade-off for your window.

Do you work with large windows?

Yes. Wide and tall openings are one of the main reasons to go custom. Depending on the span we'll recommend coupled shades on a shared headrail, a panel track system, vertical blinds, or a motorised solution.

Do you cover patio doors?

Yes. Panel track blinds, vertical blinds and drapery on a traverse track all work on sliding and patio doors — the main consideration is where the stack sits so the doorway stays clear.

How long does the process take?

It depends on the product, the size of the order and current lead times from our suppliers, so we won't quote a number here. You'll get a specific timeline in writing with your quote, before you commit to anything.

Do you offer commercial solutions?

Yes. We work with offices, condos, restaurants, retail spaces, property managers, builders and contractors on commercial and contract projects.

What areas do you service?

Please contact us with your address and we'll confirm whether you're inside our service area. We'd rather tell you honestly up front than take a booking we can't fulfil.

How do I request a quote?

Use the quote form on this site, or call or email us. Tell us the number of windows, roughly what you're looking for, and any measurements or photos you already have — we'll come back to you with options.

Do you offer warranty and support?

Warranty terms depend on the product and the component manufacturer, so we won't state a blanket period here. You'll receive the applicable warranty details in writing with your order, and if something needs adjusting afterwards you call us directly.

Are your blinds child- and pet-safe?

Cordless and motorised operation removes accessible operating cords, which is the main hazard. Where a corded product is used, we install the cord-safety hardware that comes with it. If you have young children or pets, tell us at the consultation and we'll steer the specification accordingly.

Can you match window coverings across a whole house?

Yes, and it's worth doing. We can colour-match across different product families so that, for example, faux wood in the bathroom and real wood in the living room read as the same finish from the street.
