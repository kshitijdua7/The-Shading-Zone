About Shading Zone

Shading Zone is a custom window covering company. We design, manufacture, supply and install blinds, shades, curtains and drapery for homes and commercial spaces.

Most window covering problems are not really about the blind. They are about a measurement somebody took in a hurry, a stock size trimmed down to nearly fit, or a fabric chosen from a photograph on a screen instead of held against the wall it has to live beside.

Shading Zone exists to remove those handoffs. We measure your windows ourselves. We manufacture to those measurements. We install what we made — and if something needs adjusting a month later, you call the people who made it rather than a call centre with no record of your order.

We believe window coverings should do more than control light. They should transform the way a space looks, feels and functions.

How It Works

Seven steps. All of them ours.

01 — Consultation We listen to your needs, style and vision. In your home or on a video call, we look at the rooms, the light and how you actually use the space, then narrow a very large catalogue down to a short list worth considering.

02 — Measurement Our team takes precise measurements of your windows. Every opening, every reveal depth, every out-of-square frame. This is the step that decides whether the finished product fits like it was made for the window — because it was.

03 — Design We help you select the perfect materials, colours, textures and functionality. Samples in your own light, against your own walls, at the time of day you actually use the room.

04 — Manufacturing Your window coverings are custom manufactured to your specifications. Cut, assembled and finished to the measurements we took.

05 — Quality Check Every order is inspected before installation. Dimensions verified, operation tested, fabric checked for flaws, hardware confirmed complete.

06 — Installation Our professional team installs everything with precision. Brackets set, coverings levelled, operation demonstrated, packaging taken away with us.

07 — Enjoy Your Space Your windows are transformed. And if something needs adjusting afterwards, you call the people who made it.

How Your Coverings Are Made

Made for your windows. Not off the shelf.

A stock blind is made to a size somebody guessed at, then trimmed to fit yours. Ours starts as a roll of fabric and a set of measurements taken at your window.

Fabric selection — pulled from the roll and inspected for flaws, shade variation and direction before anything is cut
Cutting — to your finished dimensions, square to the weave, with allowances calculated for the specific construction
Assembly — tubes, rails, tracks, cords and mechanisms matched to the size and weight of your covering, assembled by hand
Custom sizing — slat counts, fold spacing, vane counts and panel widths set from your measurements
Motorisation — where specified, the motor is fitted, powered, and its travel limits set and tested before it leaves the bench
Finishing — hems, edges, headers, valances and returns finished to the standard we'd want in our own homes
Quality control — dimensions re-checked against the order, operation cycled, fabric inspected in raking light
Packaging — wrapped and labelled by room and window, so installation day is quick and nothing gets confused on site

Frequently Asked

Do you offer free consultations? Yes. In-home and virtual consultations are free and carry no obligation.

Do you manufacture your own blinds? Yes. We design, manufacture, supply and install. Your order is built to the measurements we take at your window.

Do you provide installation? Yes. Our own installation team fits everything and takes the packaging away.

Can blinds be completely customized? Within each product family — dimensions, fabric, colour, opacity, lining, header style, hardware finish, control type and mounting are all specified per window.

Do you offer motorized blinds? Yes, across essentially every product family, with battery, plug-in and hardwired motors.

Do you offer blackout options? Yes — blackout rollers, blackout cellulars, blackout-lined romans, and lined or interlined drapery. No product blocks light at the edges unless it's outside-mounted or fitted with side channels, and we'll explain that trade-off for your window.

Do you work with large windows and patio doors? Yes. Wide and tall openings are one of the main reasons to go custom.

Are your blinds child- and pet-safe? Cordless and motorised operation removes accessible operating cords, which is the main hazard. Tell us at the consultation and we'll steer the specification accordingly.

How long does the process take? It depends on the product, the size of the order and current lead times. You'll get a specific timeline in writing with your quote, before you commit to anything.

What areas do you service? Contact us with your address and we'll confirm. We'd rather tell you honestly up front than take a booking we can't fulfil.
